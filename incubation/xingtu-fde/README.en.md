# XingTu FDE — Open Knowledge & Engineering Practice

**XingTu FDE** is an independent, source-traceable knowledge and practice system for Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE).

- **Knowledge:** delivery methods, AI engineering, architecture, industry research, and business hypotheses.
- **Practice:** field notes, transparent case studies, failure modes, acceptance evidence, and retrospectives.
- **Tools:** links to reusable Skills, prompts, specifications, playbooks, and templates maintained at their source.

Our principle: **Trace the theory. Verify the practice. Reuse the capability.**

This is not a second orchestration engine, a production deployment of DeepSeek Super Agent, or an archive of confidential customer material. Entries explicitly distinguish first-party published claims, third-party accounts, synthetic examples, and independently validated findings.

Start with the [Chinese guide](README.md), [knowledge map](docs/KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md), [source policy](docs/SOURCE_POLICY.md), [case cards](cases/CASE-001-FUND-REPORT.md), and [repository index](registry/REPOS.md).
